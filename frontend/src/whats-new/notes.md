## 0.1.0

- You can keep one shared shopping list with the people you shop with, tick items off and see who added them.
- You can compare prices for the same product across shops and see which one is cheapest today.
- You can sign in with a passkey, and add more passkeys from Settings.
- You can invite people to your household from the People page. (owner)
- You can open What's new from the account menu or the More page to see what changed.
- The app now uses the Lifekit look. You can change the look in Settings under Appearance.
- A new Plan tab shows the best shop for each thing on your list, with the price and the day it was seen, and tells you whether one shop can cover the whole list. Things it cannot price are marked, never guessed. Prices moved behind More.
